Privacy Policy
RoomRhythm Home Guide

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Scope and publisher

This Privacy Policy applies to RoomRhythm Home Guide (the “App”), published by PUBLISHER_NOT_CONFIGURED. The App is a local-only planning and guide tool. It does not require an account. Questions about this policy can be sent to SUPPORT_EMAIL_NOT_CONFIGURED.

2. Information used on your device

The App uses information that you choose to enter: space names and goals, reset steps and checkmarks, routine names and intervals, completion history, outbox item names and destinations, the active space selection, and guide bookmarks/read status. It also stores whether you loaded the editable sample workspace. These records are stored locally in the App’s private WebView storage so the features work when you return. The App does not transmit this workspace to the publisher or an App server.

3. Collection and sharing

The App has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login or tracking SDKs. The supplied Android manifest requests no permissions, including no Internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location, notification, storage-access or advertising-ID permission. The publisher does not automatically collect or receive your workspace, device identifiers or usage events through the App. The App does not sell user data or share the local workspace with third parties.

4. Optional external links and support

A reference link or public policy link opens your chosen browser only when you select it. Those websites may process the requests they receive under their own policies; the App does not attach your workspace to these links. A support-email link opens your email app. Sending an email is your choice and shares the information you include, along with normal email metadata, with the publisher and the email service providers. Do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is used to answer the request and resolve the issue; it is retained only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose or a legal obligation. You may request deletion of that correspondence through the contact above. The public policy hosting service may receive standard request metadata when its webpage is visited; this policy page includes no analytics or tracking scripts.

5. Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you delete them through an available item-level control, use Settings → Delete all local app data, clear this App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. Deleting a primary record can also remove associated records as explained by the in-app confirmation. Some historical counts or activity records remain after an individual item is removed; deleting all local app data removes the entire workspace. This version offers no cloud synchronisation, export, account or account-deletion process. The supplied Android configuration disables backup and excludes app data from cloud backup and device transfer; behaviour can depend on the operating system or device manufacturer. The App provides no recovery service, and independently made copies are outside its control.

6. Security and your choices

Local data uses Android’s normal application isolation. This App does not add its own database encryption or claim end-to-end encryption. Device compromise, an unlocked shared device or independently created copies may expose information. Use your device’s security controls, avoid entering sensitive material, and keep independent copies of irreplaceable information. The App limits embedded content to bundled assets, does not offer a JavaScript-to-native data bridge, and blocks embedded network requests. External browsers and email applications operate separately.

7. Audience and children

This release is designed and marketed for adult users and is not directed to children. The publisher must select a truthful target audience in Google Play before release. The App does not create accounts or knowingly transmit children’s information to the publisher. If a child sends personal information by support email, contact the publisher to request appropriate handling or deletion. A future child-directed release would require a separate policy and compliance review.

8. Platform services and changes

Android, Google Play, the device manufacturer and external applications may process their own operational data under their own terms. This policy describes the supplied App, not those independent services. Changes to the App’s data practices require this policy and the Google Play Data safety answers to be reviewed before the changed version is published. The effective date identifies this version.
